### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — `init` and `setup mcp` crashed on a platform other than macOS, Linux or Windows.** The agent-config planner looks paths up in a `darwin`/`linux`/`win32` table, and its callers cast `process.platform` to one of those keys. On Android (Termux), FreeBSD or OpenBSD the lookup returned `undefined` and `path.join` threw `ERR_INVALID_ARG_TYPE`, so `init` stopped after writing the wiring and before starting the dev server. `reticle mcp` hit the same throw in its approval migration, which caught it and skipped the migration without a word. A new `platformKey` maps `darwin` and `win32` to themselves and every other platform to `linux`, whose home layout they share, and both callers use it. Closes [#1675](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1675).
