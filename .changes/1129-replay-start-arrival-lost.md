### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server`: a replay whose tab does not come back after the start-page reload ends as `unverifiable`, instead of timing out on a dead session.** Replay reloads the tab onto the flow's `startPath` and waits for the SDK to reconnect. When nothing reconnected in time (a slow app, or a throttled or background tab), replay carried on with the session handle the page had just torn down. Every step's `query` then failed with `command 'query' timed out` while the live tools, on the same page, answered fine. Replay now stops before step 1 and says the page did not reconnect, and a navigation the browser refused still continues on the untouched tab as before. Closes [#1129](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1129).
