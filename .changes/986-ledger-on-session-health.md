### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` — the session health block names the event ledger once it starts to fill.** A repeating uncaught error wrote one session's `events.jsonl` at 13 MB/s for 14 hours and filled a 926 GB disk; neither the daemon nor `doctor` nor any health counter said a word, and the user found out when the disk was full. A cap now refuses the write, but a ledger quietly approaching it was still invisible. `health.ledger` reports the bytes on disk and the ceiling they are measured against, present only past a quarter of the cap so a healthy session carries nothing. Part of [#986](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/986).
