### Changed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — the daemon syncs when `.reticle` changes, not once a minute.** It watches the whole `.reticle` folder and runs one sync cycle about 2 seconds after a change, so a drive that writes many files costs one cycle. A change during a running cycle gets exactly one follow-up. The sync's own bookkeeping (`cloud-state.json`, `issues.json`) and the per-event session journal never wake it. The idle timer is now 10 minutes and stays as the backstop: it pulls decisions made on the dashboard and syncs the other linked folders on the machine. A failed sync does not wait for it: the daemon retries after 1 minute, doubles the wait after each further failure up to 10 minutes, and goes back to the 10-minute rhythm after a sync succeeds. A change still syncs within about 2 seconds while it is retrying. Where the folder cannot be watched (an unsupported filesystem, too many files), the daemon logs `reticle_cloud_watch_unavailable` once and syncs on the timer.
