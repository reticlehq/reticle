### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server`: `reticle try <url>`.** One watched Harness drive on your own running app, with no SDK and no change to your repository: a visible browser opens on the URL, the Harness explores for a couple of minutes, and the terminal lists each journey it tried as working, broken, or not proved, with a link to the run in your dashboard. It needs a signed-in, linked machine (`reticle connect`, free); a workspace whose free drive is spent is told how to start its trial.
