### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server`: a saved Harness plan runs again replay first, and only pays the model where a replay cannot decide.** When the platform serves a journey as replays of the flows that passed last time, with its goal kept as a fallback, the daemon replays them with no model. Only when every replay of that journey decides nothing (drifted, unverifiable, or the flow is not on this computer) does the model drive the goal; a replay that fails is reported as a regression and is never re-driven. If the Harness may not drive at that moment (credits gone, switched off), the journey is blocked with that reason. Each journey's result now tells the platform whether it was replayed or driven, the model tokens and credits it spent, and the flows it replayed or recorded; the run's answer, `journeys` (`by`, `tokens`) and the HUD's Harness log say the same per journey ("replayed, no model tokens" or "driven by the model, N tokens"). Tokens a replay saved are not printed: nothing measured them.
