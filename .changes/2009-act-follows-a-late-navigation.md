### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a sign-in whose redirect answered after the act budget read as a failed act on the old page.** An act that causes a full-page navigation (a form POST that 302s, the most common sign-in shape) spent the budget waiting while the navigation was still in flight; the verdict then graded the pre-navigation document — "current route '/login', expected '/today'" — for a sign-in that worked, and the lease's own reconnect on /today did not change it. A failed verdict now waits a bounded grace for the document's successor (a pool lease re-stamps its marker, so the successor claims the same identity), re-asks the consequence there, and when the successor never connects says exactly that — the page left its document and no successor connected — instead of leaving the failure standing. Callers that grant no grace keep today's behaviour. Closes [#2009](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/2009).
