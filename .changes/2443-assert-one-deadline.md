### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — `reticle_assert` could run for two times its `timeout_ms`.** The call gave the full budget to the wait for a session, and then the full budget again to the wait for the predicate. When the app connected late, the client stopped waiting first. `reticle verify --expect` then printed a raw "Request timed out" error, and a verdict that the daemon reached was lost. Now `timeout_ms` is one deadline for the whole call: the predicate gets only the time that the session wait left. When the app connects during the call and the check then fails, the verdict is `unknown` and says how many ms were left, because the check ran on a page that had only just connected. If the final call still times out, `--expect` prints `verified: unknown`, says how long it waited, and tells you to use a longer `--timeout`. Closes [#2443](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/2443).
