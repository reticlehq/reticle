### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server`: a full lease pool made `reticle drive` hang for 60 s and then claim the daemon was shutting down.** Once every lease slot was taken (by parallel agents, for example), a new `reticle_lease` acquire or `reticle drive` queued behind the cap with no deadline and no status. After 60 s the CLI gave up and reported "it answered a moment ago, so it is shutting down", although the daemon was healthy. Agents retried in a loop and never got a session. A lease or drive request against a full pool is now refused at once: "all N lease slots are in use. Release one (`reticle_lease{action:"release"}`, or stop a `reticle drive`), or raise `RETICLE_MAX_CONTEXTS`." If the CLI does time out on a busy daemon, it now says the daemon is running and busy instead of saying it stopped. Closes [#1535](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1535).
