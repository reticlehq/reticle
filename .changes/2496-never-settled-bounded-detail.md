### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/engine` — a `request-never-settled` finding listed every pending request.** On a page with a map, one finding named hundreds of tile requests, so the result got too long, the client cut it, and the verdict was hard to find. The detail now names at most five calls. Calls that share an origin and a path prefix are grouped, for example `GET https://tile.openstreetmap.org/12/2048/1361.png and 299 more under GET https://tile.openstreetmap.org/12/`. A pending write is always named before the reads. Each name is clipped at 200 characters. The list of ignored third-party calls has the same limits. When the detail leaves calls out, the finding has `omitted` (how many it does not name). When it leaves calls out or clips a name, the finding has `truncated: true`. Closes [#2496](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/2496).
