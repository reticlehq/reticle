### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — after one flow left the app, `reticle verify` lost the verdicts of the flows after it, and blamed the port.** A flow that went to a page without the Reticle SDK (for example a server's 404 page) ended the browser session. The next flows then ran with no session. Each one waited, and then got the generic "check your port" reason, although the port and the SDK setup had worked a moment before. Now `verify` opens the start URL again after such a flow, so the next flows get their own verdicts. The flow that lost the session is reported as not proved. Its reason starts with `no session after navigation`, names the URL where the session was lost, and then gives the reason that the replay gave. The next flow tries once, and its replay waits for its start page as every replay does, so a slow dev server has time to load the page. If that flow also gets no session, the later flows are not replayed. Their reason names the flow and the URL where the session was lost. Another connected tab, for example your own, is not reported as a lost session. Closes [#2060](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/2060).
