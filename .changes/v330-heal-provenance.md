A healed step records that it was healed. `heal` rewrote a step's anchor and left nothing behind, so a locator a machine rebound became byte-indistinguishable from one a person recorded by driving.

Three things went with that, and all of them matter later rather than at the moment of the heal. A replay passing on a healed anchor is a weaker claim than one passing on a recorded anchor, because the rebind chose the element and the consequence only checked that something still satisfied it. A step healed twice is a locator that is not stable, which was unknowable because the second heal overwrote the first. And a human reading the diff saw a changed testid with nothing saying who changed it.

`healed.from` is the ORIGINAL recorded anchor and survives later heals, because the intermediate names were never chosen by anybody. Optional and additive: a flow without the field reads exactly as before, which is every flow recorded until now, and no file version moves for it.
