### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server`: one burst of events no longer makes every later verdict on the tab `unclean_capture`.** Above its per-second cap the bridge samples events and records how many it dropped, but that count was a running total for the whole connection and every later `act_and_wait` and `assert` read it as a gap in its own window, so a live chart or a busy animation early in a session left the tab unable to give a clean verdict until it reconnected. Drops are now kept by the second they happened in, and only drops inside a verdict's window impeach it, which is how the browser-side transport gap was already read. A window that does contain drops is still `unclean_capture`, and the coverage note still says the session was sampled. Closes [#1414](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1414).
