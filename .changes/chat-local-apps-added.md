### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` + the platform: the chat knows which of your apps it can drive.** Every app on your machines with Reticle connected is listed in the platform's chat, live or greyed, with a Sync that asks its machine to open it again; a new chat opens already attached to the app you have running. Two projects on the same `localhost` port are two apps with two names, because an app is identified by its project on its machine, never by its port, and a conversation keeps naming the app it drove after the port moves on. See [What is recorded](docs/what-is-recorded.md) for what the daemon sends.
- **`@reticlehq/server` + `@reticlehq/browser` + the platform: choose how a drive runs.** From the chat: in your tab, headless, or in a window on your screen; with the HUD shown, hidden or off; once or replayed several times to see whether it is flaky; and as a persona you name, or one the platform chooses after looking at the app. A drive the platform runs ends with a report: the persona, the plan and how each part came out, every replay (each followed by a check of the end state), where it ran, and every step's input and output. `reticle_lease` takes `headed` and `hud`, and a page Reticle opens reads `__reticle_hud`, so a drive asked for no HUD never paints one.
- **`@reticlehq/server`: the platform can grow without a new release of Reticle.** The daemon tells the platform which version of their link it speaks and what it can do, and the platform offers only that. A drive's options are read one by one: anything this daemon does not know or cannot do is reported back with the reason, and the drive still runs. A drive the platform runs is a session of Reticle tool calls the daemon executes and answers, so new kinds of drive arrive with the platform, not with an upgrade. A request lost to a network blip is sent again and answered once, so the drive carries on; the platform can tell you to update Reticle through the daemon's log and HUD.
