### Added

- **`@reticlehq/init`, `@reticlehq/server` — the episode log keeps what the agent said when it finished a turn.** Each `agent_stop` record holds the agent's final message: its first 4,000 characters with secrets removed, its full size, and whether it was cut. It comes from each agent's end-of-turn hook, where the vendor's reference (2026-10-18) gives the message: Claude Code and Codex `Stop`, Cursor `afterAgentResponse`, Gemini CLI `AfterAgent` and Windsurf `post_cascade_response`. Copilot CLI's `agentStop` and Factory Droid's `Stop` carry no message, so they are not wired. The hook answers at once and writes in the background, like the post-tool hook. Measured over 100 calls each, 400 ms apart: p50 28 ms and p95 31 ms (Cursor), p50 27 ms and p95 32 ms (Gemini CLI), not counting npx's own start. The record stays on your machine, and it is off when the journal is off.
