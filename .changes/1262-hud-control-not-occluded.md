### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/browser`: a click on a control in Reticle's own panel was reported as blocked by the app underneath it.** The hit test drops Reticle's own nodes from the point stack so our overlay never counts as covering an app control. When the target WAS one of our nodes the same filter removed the target from its own hit test, and the first app element beneath the panel — the page it floats over — was blamed for covering it. Reported through the presenter-visible hatch, which exists to let Reticle verify Reticle, the result was that every `reticle_act` on a panel control came back `occluded` and the controls the hatch exposes could not be driven at all. The target and its own subtree are now kept in the stack, where they land on the existing "the element is on top of itself" branch. An app modal genuinely covering a panel control is still reported. Closes [#1262](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1262).
