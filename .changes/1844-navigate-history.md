### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` — `reticle_navigate { history: "back" | "forward" }` presses the browser Back and Forward buttons.** An agent could not move a tab through its history: a synthetic Alt+ArrowLeft does not navigate, and a URL navigation adds an entry instead. So a bug such as "Back after a form submit shows the wrong page" could not be checked. The result names the URL where the tab arrived, and `confirmed: true` means that the tab was seen to stop there, after any route-guard redirect. Forward or Back with no entry on the page's origin is refused, and nothing waits. A leased tab whose page has no SDK moves through the browser. A production drive refuses the move, because its target cannot be checked against the origin first. The page SDK serves a new `history` command. Update the SDK in the app with the server: on a page whose SDK declares its commands and does not serve `history`, the move is refused before anything is sent. Closes [#1844](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1844).
