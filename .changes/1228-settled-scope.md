### Fixed

- **`settled` counted two kinds of traffic that every other settle decision in the product ignores, so it could never pass again after a plain link click.** A departure — the `NET_PENDING` the SDK emits for an OAuth handoff or a native download, which by construction never completes because the response goes to a document the session does not live into — was counted as outstanding, so after clicking an ordinary `<a href>` every subsequent `reticle_assert { kind: "settled" }` on that page reported "1 request(s) still in flight" forever. Foreign traffic was counted too: a vendor analytics beacon, or a same-origin endpoint the project declared in `.reticle.json` `background`, held settle open with no way to stop it, so on an app embedding a wallet SDK that POSTs telemetry continuously EVERY assertion came back `unknown / outcome_pending` no matter what feature was under test. `evalSettled` now drops the same three things the act settle-wait and the contradiction pass drop, reads the app origin and declared background list off the session, and discloses what it ignored on the evidence rather than swallowing it. Agents who had been deleting `settled` from their `until` to get a verdict at all can put it back. Closes [#1228](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1228).
