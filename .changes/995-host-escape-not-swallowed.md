### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/browser` — Escape closes your app's dialogs again while the HUD is up.** The HUD answers Escape by closing its chat, then its settings, then collapsing itself, and it cancelled the key each time. Cancelling Escape cancels the browser's close request, so a `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` stopped closing the moment a session was running — a bug the author of that modal sees only in dev, only with Reticle attached, and never in production. Library modals went the same way: the common ones decline to dismiss an event another handler has already answered, and the HUD had answered it. The HUD now stands down from a press that another handler already took, and from any press belonging to a dialog the app has open, without touching its own state on the way past — the same press must not both dismiss the app's modal and quietly collapse the HUD. Escape still closes the chat, the settings and the HUD when the app has nothing open, and still does so when the press came from inside Reticle's own UI. Refs [#995](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/995).
