### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` + the platform: drive your localhost app from the platform's chat.** Ask the chat about an app that only runs on your machine (`http://localhost:…`, or `/local`) and Reticle running on your machine drives it there: the daemon of a linked project with an app connected asks the platform for a drive the chat filed, drives the tab it picks (the one the request names, then a visible one, then the most recent), and reports each step and a verdict on the goal. The chat shows the drive live, a picture of the tab about once a second unless you asked not to be recorded, and then keeps it as a check like any hosted one: in Runs (once, with the run the daemon synced folded in), in the replay viewer, and in the chat's history. A drive is "yes" only when a check held and none failed; a goal judged unmet, or a failed check, is "no"; anything else is "unknown". Needs Reticle 3.6 or later on your machine; see [What is recorded](docs/what-is-recorded.md) for what it sends.
