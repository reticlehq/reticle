### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a recorded "fill a field, press Enter" journey failed in `reticle verify` on a working app.** When Reticle drives the browser itself, a press now goes to the real keyboard in every case. Before, a press aimed at a field stayed a synthetic key event, because a real keyboard cannot aim at an element, and a browser submits no form for a synthetic Enter. Now Reticle first focuses the field, checks that it has focus, and then presses the real key, as a leased tab already did. Replayed flows also use the real keyboard on that browser. A field that does not take focus keeps the synthetic press, and the result says why. The same applies to a press inside `reticle_act_and_wait` and inside a sequence, live and in a replayed flow: the other steps of a sequence still run together in the page, and each press uses the real keyboard.
