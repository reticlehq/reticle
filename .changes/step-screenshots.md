### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server`, `@reticlehq/browser` — every step leaves a screenshot.** `reticle_act`, `reticle_act_and_wait`, `reticle_navigate`, `reticle_assert`, each step of `reticle_act { steps }` and each replayed flow step (verify suites, Harness drives) now answer with `screenshot: { path, source }`: a JPEG of the page at the moment the outcome was decided, under `.reticle/visual/runs/<runId>/`. The path is named at once and the file lands right after, so a verdict never waits for it, and the image never enters the agent's context unless it opens it. `source` is `browser` for a real screenshot of a tab Reticle drives, or `rendered` when the page drew itself in-page — approximate, since images and fonts from other origins are left out — with password and secret-named fields masked. When no picture could be taken, the field says why. The run artifact lists every screenshot and pins each to its check or flow step, and once the platform has accepted a run, its screenshots are uploaded once each. `sync.screenshots: false` in `.reticle/cloud.json` keeps them on this machine; it follows `sync.runs` when unset. An older platform without the endpoint keeps them local and is not asked again for that run.
