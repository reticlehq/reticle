### Changed

- **The daemon's account of a failed connect leads with four sentences instead of five hundred words.** `reticle init` prints that account when no session appears, and it was one unbroken paragraph: a port-scan disclaimer, a four-item differential, a lease offer and a non-localhost aside, concatenated, with the line naming the actual fix somewhere in the middle. Measured on a real failed run it was over three thousand characters; the lead a person now reads is under a thousand, and it is the lead plus the next action.

  Nothing was deleted. Almost every clause in that paragraph was written because somebody was sent the wrong way without it, so the differential moved rather than went: `/status` carries both, and so do the `notes` in `reticle init --json`, so the agent about to spend a drive on a guess still gets all of it — including the lease, which is the only way to open a URL on a machine with no browser. The two readers can afford different lengths, and only one of them was being asked to read at a terminal.
