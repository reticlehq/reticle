### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a daemon that held the port and did not answer was reported as "port free", and `restart` could not recover.** A daemon stuck in its exit keeps its listening socket open and accepts nothing. When the connect queue is full, a connect times out. The port probe read that timeout as "nothing listens", so `reticle status` said the port was free, and the MCP proxy said "nothing is listening" and then "not a Reticle daemon". Now a loopback timeout or a reset counts as a held port (not on Windows, where a free port also times out). `status`, `serve`, `restart`, `doctor`, `drive`, `try` and the MCP proxy name the holder pid and say when its command line is a Reticle daemon. `restart` stops such a daemon, though no pid file names it, and starts a new one on the same port. The MCP proxy does the same when it starts and when it wakes, and does not move to a port that the app's SDK does not dial. The daemon's exit now has a 10-second bound, so a close that never finishes cannot keep the process on its port. Closes [#1937](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1937).
