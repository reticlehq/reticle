### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/engine` — one clause that could not be read made the whole `allOf` or `anyOf` `unknown`.** A check expected `POST /api/auth/login` to answer 401, and it answered 500. A text clause in the same `allOf` timed out on a slow page. The verdict was `unknown`, with "the page did not answer: command 'match' timed out", and the 500 was lost. The cause: `allOf` and `anyOf` read their clauses together, and one read that threw rejected all of them. Now a clause whose read throws is inconclusive by itself, and it keeps its reason. The composite rules then decide. In `allOf`, a clause that failed outranks a clause that nobody could read, so this check is `no` at the end of the budget. In `anyOf`, a clause that passed is a pass. When no clause failed or passed, the verdict is still `unknown`, with the reason of the read that threw. A one-shot check (`timeout_ms: 0`) and the pre-check before an action now get this verdict too, where before they got a tool error. No timing rule changes. Closes [#1656](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1656).
