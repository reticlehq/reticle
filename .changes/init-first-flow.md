### Changed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — `reticle init` ends with what Reticle sees, not a drive.** Once the app connects, init prints its instrumentation score, what is missing, whether Reticle Harness can be switched on, and a prompt for your coding agent that closes the gaps and proves the first flow. It never drives. Unlinked projects are told once that `reticle connect` keeps results on the dashboard and lets the Harness test the whole app (10 free credits). `--no-first-run` stops after the connection.
- **Reticle Harness is opt-in and unlocks at 80% instrumentation.** A new project starts with the Harness off. Below 80% (seen ÷ applicable over the eight instrumentation capabilities), `reticle_verify {action:"explore"}`, Run Harness in the HUD and `reticle try` refuse with one sentence naming the percent and what is missing, plus the prompt that closes it; the HUD shows the lock and a "Copy prompt for your coding agent" button, and Settings locks the switch. Every other tool works at any coverage.
- **HUD credits.** Signed out: "Sign up: 10 free credits to try Reticle Harness · add a card for a 500-credit, 14-day trial". Signed in: credits used and left, with days left when the platform gives an end date.
- **Agent nudges.** At most once per agent session per project per kind, and never on a failed result, a tool result tells the agent to tell the user: `reticle connect` (unlinked, after the first verdict), which gaps unlock the Harness (linked, below 80%), that the Harness can be switched on (at 80%, off), or to hand a long hand-driven journey to explore (Harness on).
