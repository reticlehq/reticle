An unproved verdict now says WHOSE problem it is. Beside `reason`, the evidence block carries `attributedTo`: one of `code`, `environment`, `harness` or `could-not-see`.

32% of verdicts are `unknown`, and the word is honest but unactionable on its own. "The backend answered 202 and has not finished", "the page SDK and the daemon are on different contracts" and "the capture was truncated" arrive as the same string and need three opposite next moves: wait, fix Reticle's setup, look again with better coverage. Each value implies a different move, and `code` - the only one that means go and change the app - is reserved for the two clauses where the app was actually disproved.

Derived from the deciding clause, so there is no second decision that can disagree with the first, and the map is exhaustive over every reason the ladder can produce: a new clause cannot ship without somebody saying who owns it. Omitted on a proof, which has no owner to name.
