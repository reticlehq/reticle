### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/engine` — a canvas-rendered app could not get a `yes` for a state change.** A canvas repaint makes no DOM mutation, so every correct state change in an editor, a chart or a game was graded `state-vs-render` ("the store committed and nothing rendered"). The rule now reads the canvas digest the browser SDK already sends, which now says whether the canvas was on screen: a visible canvas that held its picture for a second before the action, did not change on its own in the 10 seconds before it, and then shows a change is the screen moving. When a visible canvas shows no change the action caused (the same picture, a spinner, or a chart or clock that refreshes on a timer), the rule does not fire, and the `act_and_wait` coverage line says `canvas paint not observed`. A canvas on a timer slower than 10 seconds can still read as the render. A hidden or off-screen canvas is ignored. A DOM app that does not re-render still gets `state-vs-render`. Closes [#1525](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1525).
