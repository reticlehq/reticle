### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` — a flow says at save whether it can replay, and the suite says it again.** Of 34 flows saved over one night, 5 replayed green, and none of the misses was a defect in the app. Each save now answers `replayability`: `not_replayable` (no starting page, an anchor no replay can find, an anchor several elements share), `data_bound` (live data in an expect, such as a count or a balance), and `writes` when a step sends a write request. A live count in a text expect ("Open 336") is saved word-level: the words stay exact and the number may change. A money check keeps its number. A step whose expect names a POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE is marked `writes: true`. `reticle_verify { action: "flows" }` lists each flow that cannot replay as saved, and `skipWrites: true` holds back every flow that would change real data, named in `skippedWrites`.
