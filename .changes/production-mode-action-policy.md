### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server`: a production action policy for drives on a live site.** On a session that a production nonce opened, the daemon judges every command where it is dispatched, never in the page. Scope `observe` refuses every act. Scope `act` refuses payment or checkout, deleting a workspace or project, revoking or rotating a key, sending an invite or email, cancelling a subscription and changing a member's role, unless the drive's declared intent names that action. The daemon recognises them by the control (its name, text, value, test id, link and form target) and by the request (method and path, from a policy list the session supplies, with `DEFAULT_PRODUCTION_POLICY` as the default). Navigation on the activated origin, reading, filters and panels are allowed, and so is deleting what the drive itself created (ids it saw come back from its own 2xx creates). Every refusal is a failed result that names the rule: `refused: production policy: <rule>`. Native (CDP) gestures go through the same rule.
