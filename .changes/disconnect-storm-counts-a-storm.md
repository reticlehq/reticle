### Fixed

- **The MCP proxy no longer leaves after a working week of ordinary daemon restarts.** Its disconnect counter was declared once and never reset, so twenty disconnects spread over days reached the same threshold as twenty in eight seconds — and crossing it makes the proxy exit, which a user experiences as Reticle disappearing mid-session with nothing said. The classifier behind the count reads `ECONNRESET` as "the peer vanished", and on the proxy's connection to the daemon the peer _is_ the daemon: the thing the proxy exists to survive, and a thing that restarts routinely. Reaching an `endpoint` frame now clears the count, which is the same signal that already earns a fresh retry budget and was chosen for the same reason — a daemon that accepts a stream and drops it still produces response headers, and does not produce an `endpoint` frame. The threshold now measures a runaway rather than an uptime.
