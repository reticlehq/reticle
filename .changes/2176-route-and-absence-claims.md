### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/engine`, `@reticlehq/server`, `@reticlehq/browser` — a passing route claim, or a dialog asserted absent by testid, came back `unknown`.** A click that did a full navigation, with a `route` predicate that passed, got `action-had-no-effect` when the window held no events. Now a route clause that held, and that was not true before the action, counts as an effect of the click. A click on an inert button with a `settled` wait still gets `action-had-no-effect`. On a page with a virtualized list, a dialog asserted absent by testid got `absence_blind_spot`, because a row that was not rendered could hold the target. The SDK now records the testid of a removed element. When the window holds the removal of the asserted testid, and the removed element is a dialog or a landmark, the blind spot does not apply. A row of the virtualized list asserted absent stays `unknown`, also by its testid. Closes [#2176](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/2176).
