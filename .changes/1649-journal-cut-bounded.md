### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/server` — one cut journal read made every later verdict `unknown`.** When the session journal grew past its read bound between two reads, the read skipped bytes and recorded the loss with no time. From then on, every `reticle_act_and_wait` and `reticle_assert` on that session gave `unknown` / `unclean_capture` with `buffer_loss`, also for a new window with nothing lost. The cut now has a bound: the `t` of the first record after it, or the time of the read when no record is left. A window that opens after the bound is not impeached. A window that the skipped bytes can reach is still `unknown`. When the page clock can have started again across the cut (a reload, a new document, or `t` or `seq` that went down), the cut stays unbounded as before. Closes [#1649](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1649).
