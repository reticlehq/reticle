### Added

- **`@reticlehq/server` — `reticle verify --results-json` reports a score, and `--sweep` judges every step.** Each journey in the results now has `steps: { passed, failed, unknown }`, and the file has a `score` with the totals and `value` (passed divided by total). A step that the replay never reached, or could not decide, is `unknown`, and it stays in the total, so it can never raise the score. The total comes from the saved flow, so a replay that stopped at step 2 of 10 counts 8 unknown steps. With `--sweep`, the replay continues after a step whose action ran but whose consequence did not hold, so more steps get a verdict. The score is gated: a step counts only when every earlier step of its journey passed. Verdicts after the first failed step are reported apart, as `swept`, and they stay `unknown` in the score, so `--sweep` never raises it. A step whose control was not found still stops the flow. When a daemon already owns the port, verify refuses `--sweep` and does not ignore it. A step that is a sequence counts each of its sub-steps, so a recorded journey of nine sub-steps that gets seven right scores seven of nine, not zero. The file now carries `schemaVersion: 2`; a file without it is the earlier shape with only `verdict` and `results`.
