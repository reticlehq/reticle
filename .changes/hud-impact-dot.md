### Changed

- **`@reticlehq/browser` — the HUD's Impact tab shows a dot for new results instead of a count beside its name.** The count grew with the number, and at four digits "Impact" ran into the next tab's name. A dot on the icon's top-right corner now means "new since you last opened Impact"; opening Impact clears it, per project and across reloads, and new results bring it back. The numbers stay inside the Impact panel. With browser storage blocked, no dot is shown.
