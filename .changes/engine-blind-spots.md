### Added

- **`@reticlehq/core`, `@reticlehq/server`, `@reticlehq/browser` — a verdict now says what the engine could not see in the run.** A host could read a run where nothing disagreed as `clean`, even when the defect was in pixels on a canvas, or in an amount the claim states ("each hit adds one point"). No channel here reads either. Every `reticle_act_and_wait` and `reticle_assert` result now carries `engineBlindSpots`, cumulative for the session: `pixels-unobserved` once an action ran with a `<canvas>` on screen, and `claim-arithmetic-unchecked` while a claim under test states an amount and no verdict ran a `compare`. The last verdict of a run carries the run's list. The act effect reports `canvases`, the count of canvases on screen. The kinds and the zod schema are `EngineBlindSpotKind` and `EngineBlindSpotSchema` in `@reticlehq/core`. Hosts that do not read the field see no other change.
